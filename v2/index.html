Austin & Carrie Stanton

Carrie and Austin have always had a passion for design, construction, and creating a unique home.

Their first project was turning a 1920s home in Montrose, used as a multi-office space, into their own home. Challenges came up and were overcome, and that is when they knew what they wanted to do.

They both come from a long line of real estate pioneers. Carrie’s father, James L. Robertson, a Houston real estate attorney, developed the Hearthstone neighborhood. Today they manage a portfolio of commercial and residential properties, and Stanton Realty Investments brings their construction, design and reputation together.

Three generations in Houston real estate

  1. 1950s
