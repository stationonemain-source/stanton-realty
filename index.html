Austin & Carrie Stanton

Carrie and Austin have always had a passion for design, construction, and creating a unique home.

Their first project was turning a 1920s home in Montrose, used as a multi-office space, into their own home. Challenges came up and were overcome, and that is when they knew what they wanted to do.

They both come from a long line of real estate pioneers. Carrie’s father, James L. Robertson, a Houston real estate attorney, developed the Hearthstone neighborhood. Today Carrie and Austin manage a portfolio of commercial and residential properties, and Stanton Realty Investments brings their construction, design and reputation together.

  1. 1950s

    Austin’s grandmother, Ellen English, becomes the first woman in Houston to start her own real estate company.

  2. 2001

    Carrie and Austin’s union begins. Ever since, they have worked to create their own personal spaces.

  3. 2002

    Austin’s mother, Sassy Stanton, and Bill Pinckard make it their mission to bring market data and experienced wisdom to buyers and sellers, as Stanton-Pinckard Realty.

  4. 2015

    Stanton Realty Investments is founded, a life-long dream of bringing both of their skill sets together.
